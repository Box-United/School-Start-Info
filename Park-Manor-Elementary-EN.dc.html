Train your body. Strengthen your mind.

Coaches teach real boxing fundamentals — footwork, form, strength and conditioning — with no contact and no sparring. Nobody gets hit. Girls also work through the Fighter's Mindset, our social-emotional curriculum on confidence, focus and handling stress. All equipment is provided.

“When I box, I use positive thoughts like a shield. I tell myself, ‘Don't give up. I'm strong. I can do this.'”
Kat, Age 10 · FLAG Student
