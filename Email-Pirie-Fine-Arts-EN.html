Fight Like A Girl is coming to Pirie Fine Arts
Fight Like A Girl is Box United's after-school program for girls. Coaches teach real boxing fundamentals — footwork, form, strength and conditioning — with no contact and no sparring. Nobody gets hit. Alongside the training, girls work through the Fighter's Mindset, our social-emotional learning curriculum on confidence, focus, stress and self-trust. All equipment is provided, and enrollment is free.
